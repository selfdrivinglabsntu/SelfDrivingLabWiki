Instructions

  1. Start by screwing the Linear Rail down onto the printer's X-axis gantry. Line the rail up along the gantry and slide a T-slot nut into the gantry's slot under each hole you intend to use, then run a screw down through the rail into each nut. Tighten them evenly, working from the middle outwards rather than end to end, so the rail pulls down flat. Slide the carriage along its full travel and check it runs smoothly before you build anything onto it.
  2. Now take the Frame to the bench, open side of the "C" facing you. It gives you three shelves to work between: the top plate, bored for the stepper motor; the middle shelf, with a small bore for the lead screw and a large bore for the pipette; and the bottom shelf, which carries the lower end of the lead screw. The leg below the bottom shelf is what clamps onto the linear rail.
  3. Feed the M8 x 150mm screw through the bottom shelf, through the middle shelf up to top plate until its lower end sits in the bottom shelf. Use a Flanged Self Lubricating Bushing to make ensure that the screw rotates smoothly. Slide the Driver Gear such that it sits on the middle shelf. No need to screw in the Driver Gear just yet. PLEASE IGNORE THE SCREW IN THE DRIVER GEAR IN FIGURE 4.
  4. Secure the Frame to the linear rail carriage using M3 x 25mm screws.
  5. Place the Frame Linear Actuator Mount on top of the top plate. Place the stepper motor on top of the top plate and bring the shaft coupler up to note down which position to screw in the shaft coupler to the stepper motor. Then place the combined module through the holes and screw in the M8 screw to the shaft coupler.
