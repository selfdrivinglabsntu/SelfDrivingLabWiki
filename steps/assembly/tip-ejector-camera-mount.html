Fit the tip ejector mechanism and mount the onboard camera.

Instructions

  1. First attach the Tip Ejector to the Tip Disposal. Use the bottom 4 holes and 4x M5 20mm screws.
  2. Next, attach the Camera Mount, use 2x M5 screws of length anywhere from 20mm to 40mm.
  3. Finally, attach the USB Camera, use 4x M3 4mm screws.
  4. The assembly shpuld look like this.
  5. You will need a USB extension for your USB Camera if the cable is too short to reach your computer. A short cable carries the RISK of dragging the laptop along with it when the bed plate moves. Pro-tip: one of our extensions was faulty and was giving us an out of focus image, replace the extension if you face the same problem.
